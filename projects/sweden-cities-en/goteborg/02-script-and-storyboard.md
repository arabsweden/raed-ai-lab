# Göteborg (EN): script and storyboard

## 3. Script (117 spoken words; 18–22 per scene, the length that fit 10 s in the Stockholm test)
1. **(Hook)** This building looks like a church. But it was built in 1874 to sell fish. Locals call it the Fish Church.
2. **(Location)** This is Göteborg, Sweden's second-largest city, on the west coast where the Göta älv river meets the sea.
3. **(History)** In 1621, King Gustav the Second Adolf founded the city, and Dutch builders gave it canals on marshland.
4. **(Haga)** Then walk to Haga, a historic neighbourhood, for a cinnamon bun said to be the biggest in the world.
5. **(Liseberg)** In winter, Liseberg amusement park becomes a Christmas town, with millions of lights and about two thousand Christmas trees.
6. **(Tip + CTA)** My tip: climb Skansen Kronan at sunset. I'm Raed Shihab. Which Swedish city should I explore next? Tell me below.

Spoken words per scene: 22 · 18 · 19 · 19 · 19 · 20.

## 4. Fonts and colours
Same system as Stockholm (`lang/en.md`): Anton for numbers, Unbounded for the hook, Outfit for keywords, Playfair Display for history, Caveat for nature and food, DM Serif Display for the tip, Montserrat and Barlow Condensed for Swedish labels, Bebas Neue for the signature.
Accent for this city: **sea blue #1B6FA8** plus gold #D4AF37. Christmas red #C8102E is for scene 5 only.
