# Malmö (EN): script and storyboard

## Script (118 spoken words)
1. **(Hook)** This skyscraper twists a full ninety degrees from bottom to top. It's called Turning Torso, and it opened in 2005.
2. **(Location)** This is Malmö, Sweden's third-largest city, in the far south, facing Denmark across the Öresund strait.
3. **(Bridge)** Since 2000, the Öresund Bridge has linked Sweden and Denmark. It runs almost eight kilometres, then disappears into a tunnel.
4. **(Old town)** Walk to Lilla torg, a cosy square from the 1590s, full of half-timbered houses and outdoor cafés.
5. **(Cold bath)** Brave enough? At Ribersborgs kallbadhus, a bathhouse from 1898, you take a sauna, then jump into the sea, all year round.
6. **(Tip + CTA)** My tip: watch the sunset from Daniaparken in Västra hamnen. I'm Raed Shihab. Which Swedish city next? Tell me below.
