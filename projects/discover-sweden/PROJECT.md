## Trigger
RAED enters a Swedish city name (e.g. «Malmö» or «Discover Sweden: Malmö»).

## Deliverables per episode (all prepared for RAED's review)
1. **Vertical 9:16 episode, ~60 s**: 6 scenes, hook in the first 3 s, one CTA at the end.
2. **Verified English script**: every fact with an official source link and the check date. Unverified items are dropped or marked «unverified».
3. **Scene plan with footage source**, in this order of preference:
   1. **RAED's existing footage first**: his own filmed clips, then clips already generated for earlier episodes.
   2. **A new Google Flow prompt** only for scenes with no suitable footage.
   Each scene states its source: `existing: <file>` or `new: Flow prompt`.
4. **Swedish pronunciation notes**: each Swedish name with a respelling for the TTS, plus a fallback spelling.
5. **Facebook caption**: hook line, 2–3 verified facts with emoji, one CTA, 4 hashtags. Facebook is the main platform. TikTok and Snapchat captions are optional.
6. Voice text and style instructions for AI Studio, and the cover idea.
