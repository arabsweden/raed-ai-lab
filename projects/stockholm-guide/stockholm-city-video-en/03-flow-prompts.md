## 7. Voice-over for Google AI Studio
**Link:** https://aistudio.google.com/generate-speech · **Model:** Gemini TTS, single speaker · **Voice:** Charon (informative, male) or Orus (firm), or RAED's approved English voice. Listen to a one-line sample first; voice names in the dropdown can change.

**Style instructions:**
```
Read in clear, natural English with a neutral international accent, like a confident travel-reel narrator. Brisk but relaxed pace of about 155 words per minute. Very short pauses at commas, a short beat at full stops, a slightly longer beat between paragraphs. Do not add or skip any word. Pronounce Swedish place names exactly as a native Stockholm Swedish speaker would.
```

**Text (paste as is):**
```
In sixteen twenty-eight, a brand-new royal warship sailed out of this harbour. Minutes later, she sank. Three hundred and thirty-three years later, she came back up.

This is Stockholm, Sweden's capital, spread across fourteen islands where Lake Mälaren meets the Baltic Sea. Wherever you walk here, the water walks with you.

Her name is Vasa. Ninety-eight percent of her is original, and today more than a million visitors a year come to meet her.

Across the water lies Gamla stan, the old town, where Stockholm was founded in twelve fifty-two. Its narrowest alley is just ninety centimetres wide.

Now go underground. More than ninety of the hundred metro stations have art, which is why it's billed as the world's longest art exhibition.

My tip: walk Monteliusvägen at sunset and watch the old town glow. I'm Raed Shihab. Which Swedish city should I explore next? Tell me below.
```
> 147 words, so expect 55–60 s. Numbers are spelled out the way they are spoken. Swedish names stay in Swedish spelling.

**Pronunciation check (listen for these):**

| Mälaren | MEH-lah-ren | Mehlaren |
| Gamla stan | GAHM-lah stahn | Gahmla stahn |
| Monteliusvägen | mon-TEH-lee-us-veh-gen | Montehlius-vehgen |

Check the voice against the script word by word before you approve it (production rule: no extra or missing words).
