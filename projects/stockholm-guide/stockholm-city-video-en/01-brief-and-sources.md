## 1. Summary
Hook: in 1628 a brand-new royal warship sailed out of Stockholm's harbour and sank minutes later; 333 years later she came back up.
Then the location: 14 islands where Lake Mälaren meets the Baltic Sea.
Payoff: her name is Vasa, she is 98% original, and more than a million people a year visit her.
Then Gamla stan (founded 1252) and its 90 cm alley, then the metro, billed as the world's longest art exhibition.
Close: sunset tip at Monteliusvägen, the RAED SHIHAB signature and one CTA.

**Corrections and dropped claims:**
- **«Most visited museum in Scandinavia» dropped.** Only the museum says this, and Visit Sweden says «one of the most visited in Sweden». Replaced with the hard number: more than a million visitors a year.
- **«Sweden's mightiest warship» became «a brand-new royal warship».** «Mightiest» was not verified.
- **«Sank 1,300 metres from the dock» not used.** Sources disagree: about 1,300 m, «less than a nautical mile», and one says 200 m.
- **«Exactly 20 minutes» became «minutes later».** It is a museum figure for a 17th-century event. The softer wording is safer.
- **«57 bridges» dropped.** Only travel listicles say this; not needed.
- **«110 km of art» kept off screen.** Visit Stockholm says 110 km, but other sources say 105.7 km.
- **«Sweden's narrowest alley» became «its narrowest alley» (Gamla stan's).** Visit Sweden pages disagree between «Sweden's» and «Old Town's».
- **«30,000-island archipelago» saved for another episode.** It is tourism-board wording, and the video is already 6 scenes.
